Quotes & jobs
A quote is built on a phone in her living room. A job is corrected on a laptop that evening. The quote is the first surface with two audiences, so it uses the new audience mode from the design system: his view is light (the app's normal theme) with an amber band, her view is dark and laid out differently. Phone 390, laptop 1440. API gaps are in API-gap-report-quotes-jobs.md.

The cost wall
The signal is brightness, not colour. His view is the app's normal light theme, with an amber band. Hers fills the screen dark. Across a room, in glare or at dusk, a bright phone and a dark phone are the easiest thing to tell apart, and the moment it goes dark he knows his figures are gone.
The two views have different layouts. His is dense rows, room chips and a money bar. Hers is five large lines and a total. You can tell them apart by shape before you can read a word.
Switching is easy one way and hard the other. Showing her is one tap, because going to the safe view is never dangerous. Coming back is a 900 ms hold, so a brush of her thumb can't reveal his costs.
Her view is built from her data only. It renders from /customer-view, which has no cost field to show. Nothing is hidden by styling.
In his view, dark means "hers". So his money stays light with an amber edge; the usual dark money card isn't used here, unlike the laptop. The only dark things in his view are the "On her quote" edge and the Show her button, which looks like the screen it opens.
Building
Adding is pinned to a room. The "Adding to" header is sticky, and the picker stays open for several taps. Tapping the same product again adds one to the existing line. Rooms come from the house record and are never typed here.
By room / By need is one list grouped two ways. By need is the sanity check: "Lighting · 9 points".
Tap a line to edit it. Quantity is a 48 px stepper. Need is the eight fixed chips: the group default is dashed, his choice is solid. The note is one field, empty by default.
Stock shows as you tap. A line he can't cover from stock gets an amber line with the lead time, from the line's stock_warning.
Removed lines are switched off, not deleted. They drop out of every total and out of her view. The dashed "off" button at the top shows them with their date, and each has a Switch on.
The two misc numbers can't blur. They sit in separate boxes: a dark edge for "On her quote", an amber edge for "Yours only". Materials is dashed while it's derived from the estimate, and solid once he types it.
Labour is empty until he types it. If he leaves it empty, the bar says so in amber, because send would refuse it.
No signal: the header switches to "held on phone" (Tweaks → offline). Nothing else changes, so he never has to wait on the network.

"Open" splits into "On site" and "Booked". On-site jobs come first because each one is waiting to be recorded in C. Booked jobs follow, soonest first.
"Owes you" cuts across every status. This is the API's outstanding=true filter. Jobs are sorted with the biggest balance first. An overpaid job sits in its own group, in amber, because there he owes her.
